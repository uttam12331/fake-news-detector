How it works

Your text is cleaned, turned into TF-IDF features (word importance scores), and scored by a Logistic Regression model trained on ~20,800 labelled news articles from the Kaggle "Fake News" dataset. The model reports a probability, which we show as a confidence score, and we surface the words that pushed the verdict each way.

Staying current

A model only knows what it was trained on, so this one is updatable: it folds in recent (2026) labelled news, and you can teach it by pressing Real / Fake under any result. Re-running update_model.py retrains it on the base data plus everything new.

Honest limitations

  • It judges style, not truth. It learned the writing patterns of one 2017-era dataset — it cannot fact-check a claim. A well-written lie can read as "Real".
  • Full articles only. Short headlines lack signal; you'll see a warning below ~25 words.
  • English only, and biased toward the topics/period of its training data.
